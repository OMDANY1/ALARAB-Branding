# Brand DNA

AL ARAB is not just a shawarma restaurant.

It represents Saudi craftsmanship.

The experience is built around respect for the product.

Food is the hero.

Everything should communicate precision.

The brand should feel timeless.

Never trendy.

Never loud.

Never playful.
